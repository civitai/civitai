ANGLE: Does the plan do what was asked, in the asker's words, and nothing they did not ask for?

Give this critic the requester's words verbatim (messages, dictation and answers, with where each was said; the task text; teammates' words from the meeting or thread), not the author's summary.

LANE_TASKS:
1. Map each thing asked for to the part of the plan that delivers it. List anything missing, deferred without saying so, or delivered as something quieter or cheaper.
2. List anything the plan adds that nobody asked for, including additions that came from earlier review rounds, and whether it changes what users or moderators see, or costs the requester attention (asks, approvals). Each one goes to a separate follow-up task unless the requester asked for it.
3. Check every premise used to depart from the words (a cost, a risk, "they would not want"). Measured or assumed? A departure on an unmeasured premise is a must-fix.
4. Does every decision that belongs to the requester or a teammate reach them as a question, and does anything user-facing turn on without one?
