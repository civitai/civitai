# Knights of New Order: the gold shop

Code: `apps/knights/src/lib/server/shop`, `apps/knights/src/routes/shop` (shop) and `routes/shop/inventory`. Main-app side: `src/pages/api/mod/knights/shop/{membership,cosmetics}.ts`, `src/server/services/knights/shop.service.ts` and `grantBuzzPurchaseMembership` in `src/server/services/subscriptions.service.ts`. Prices live in data. Gold is anchored at 20 gold = 1 Buzz (`CONVERSION.goldPerBuzz`, which the header's Buzz value reads). Prices are entered from the moderator page; the launch catalog is kept in HQ, not here.

## Data and deploy

Tables `KnightsShopItem`, `KnightsShopItemChange` and `KnightsShopPurchase` come from migration `20261210120000_knights_v2_tables` (by hand, with every other Knights table; nothing on main is altered). Order: apply it (after `20261209120000_knights_new_order_player_and_link_review`), deploy the main app (the two endpoints), then Knights. The catalog starts empty in every environment, dev included, and is filled from the moderator app, `/knights-of-new-order/shop`.

- `KnightsShopItem`: `kind` (`buzz_pack` | `cosmetic` | `membership`), the reference (`buzz`, `cosmeticId`, or `tier` + `days`), `price` in gold, `active`, `rotating`, `sortOrder`, optional `minRank`. A CHECK ties the columns to the kind. `id` is a serial integer (`KnightsShopPurchase.itemId` is an integer). Add, re-price, retire and restore items on the moderator page `/knights-of-new-order/shop` (service `apps/moderator/src/lib/server/knights/shop-catalog.service.ts`); nothing is deployed. A membership item must be 7 days. A creator cosmetic whose creator-shop listing is still in review (the main app's `getWithheldCosmeticIds`, which makes `equipCosmetic` answer 409) cannot be added or put back on sale: the page refuses with the reason, its picker greys such results, and a live item that falls back into review shows as "Hidden: cosmetic in review" in the table and preview. Each edit writes a `KnightsShopItemChange` row (field → {from, to}), which the page's audit tab lists. The main app's cosmetic shop editor (`upsertCosmeticShopItem`) refuses to list any Trophy cosmetic, and any cosmetic with no creator (`Cosmetic.createdById` null) that a `KnightsShopItem` references, active or retired. A creator cosmetic the Knights shop also sells stays listable in the main shop for Buzz.

### The moderator page

`routes/knights-of-new-order/shop` has three URL-driven views. **Items** (`?tab=items`, filters `q`, `status` = live | retired | all, `kind`) opens with today's stock (deals, rotating picks, count of items always on sale, a countdown to 00:00 UTC), then a table grouped like the Knights shop (Buzz packs, memberships, cosmetics). Add and Edit open one right-hand sheet: kind chosen first, a thumbnail picker for cosmetics, and a live "How players see it" card; an edit shows the kind-defining fields read-only and has a History tab backed by `shop/history` (one item's audit rows). **Preview** (`?tab=preview&day=`) draws one UTC day from the same rows and `stockFor` as Knights, laid out like the player view. **Audit** (`?tab=audit&item=&before=`) groups changes by UTC day with diff chips. `stockView` in the service serves both the items view (today) and the preview, so they cannot disagree.

The sheet and the retire dialog warn only when the change reaches players: `impact.ts` draws today's stock before and after with `stockFor` and names what drops out, comes in or becomes a deal, or that a new price refuses purchases in flight. A new item has no id, so its draw cannot be simulated and it gets a general warning.

- `KnightsShopPurchase`: the gold ledger. `requestKey` is the buyer's idempotency key (unique per player; the confirm dialog makes a fresh one each time it opens), `externalKey` is the server's key for the downstream grant (the `UserCosmetic.claimKey`, the membership grant key), `conversionId` links a Buzz pack to its `KnightsGoldConversion`, `dealDay` marks a deal price (one per player, item and UTC day, enforced by a partial unique index).

## Daily stock and deals

`stockFor` (`@civitai/shared/knights-shop`, also used by the moderator page's preview) is pure: for a UTC day, fixed items (`rotating = false`) are always on sale, a seeded pick of `ROTATION_SIZE` (4) of the rotating ones is, and `DEALS_PER_DAY` of that stock carry `DEAL_PERCENT` (20) off; `DEALS_PER_DAY` is 0 at launch, so nothing is discounted. The three constants live in the same shared module. The seed is the date and the item ids (FNV-1a), never row order, so every instance and every hour of the day agree, and the picks change at 00:00 UTC. A deal can be bought once per player per day; after that the card reads "Gone for today". A rank-gated item shows as locked below its rank. A creator cosmetic still in review is dropped before the draw (`loadShop`, `buyItem` and the moderator `stockView` filter before `stockFor`), so it takes no rotation slot or deal and is never on a card, and a limited sticker's price line reads "100 · 100 uses": the price is for the whole pack.

## Spending gold (`purchase.ts`, `buyItem`)

One transaction locks the `NewOrderPlayer` row (the same lock conversion and a career reset take), then in order: refuses no player, replays an existing `requestKey` (so a retry of a charged purchase gets its own answer even after the player is held, the item is retired or the price changed), refuses a hold (payout block or banned), checks the item is active and in today's stock (a cosmetic still in review is refused as `not-approved` before any gold is reserved, read through `withheldCosmetics` in `@civitai/db/knights`, whose rule `isListingDisplayable` and edited-field list `DISPLAYED_FIELDS` live in `@civitai/shared/knights-shop` and are imported by the main app's `creator-shop.data.ts`; `withheldCosmeticIds` is checked against `getWithheldCosmeticIds` in `creator-shop-withheld.test.ts`), that the price the buyer saw is today's price, the Buzz kill switch (Buzz packs only), the rank gate, the deal limit, ownership and (membership only) an active membership, then `spendable = max(0, goldEarned - goldConverted)` against the price, and writes the purchase and adds the price to `goldConverted`. Delivery follows:

- **Cosmetic**: the `UserCosmetic` row (`claimKey` = `externalKey`) is inserted in that same transaction, so a cosmetic is granted exactly when the gold is spent; its `grantedAt` is set from the row's own `createdAt`, one clock for both. Everything except a Sticker is bought once (also refused when the player already holds it from elsewhere). A Sticker is a row per purchase with `remaining` = the cosmetic's `data.uses`, so a repeat purchase adds uses; a sticker with no `uses` is unlimited and is not sold again. After commit Knights asks the main app to refresh the cosmetic caches (best effort: a failure is logged and the caches expire on their own).
- **Buzz pack**: a `KnightsGoldConversion` row is written in the same transaction and `settleConversion` runs the existing grant, with the conversion's key. An unknown outcome stays pending and `retryPendingConversions` (the tick) pays it once; a definitive Buzz rejection fails the conversion and its purchase and releases the gold. Marking the conversion paid or failed also marks its purchase.
- **Membership**: the main app is asked (`main-app.ts`, `POST /api/mod/knights/shop/membership` with `{ userId, tier, days, key }`) with the purchase's `externalKey`. A 2xx is granted; only the endpoint's own refusals are final (a 409 for an active membership, a product not available or not monthly (the product is only the pointer to the tier; the length comes from `days`); a 400 or 422 for a request the endpoint will never accept) and release the gold; anything else (401, 403, 404, 408, 429, 5xx, timeout) leaves it pending, because those are answered before the endpoint's replay check and say nothing about an earlier try and `retryPendingPurchases` (the tick, not behind the Buzz switch) sends the same key. A purchase still unconfirmed after `PENDING_PURCHASE_ALERT_ATTEMPTS` tries is logged as a `knights-shop-membership-stuck` error, and again every that many tries; its gold stays reserved because the grant may have happened.

A replay answers from the ledger: a failed purchase reports `forfeited` as it was when it failed (kept in the purchase's `grant`), a pending one reports `replayed: true`, and a held player's pending purchase stays reserved, as the tick leaves it.

Releasing gold is `releaseReservation` (`conversion/convert.ts`): gold reserved at or before a career reset's `startAt` was forfeited by the reset, so a late refusal does not give it back (the player is told); otherwise `goldConverted` drops by the price.

### Why spending adds to `goldConverted`

There is no new column. `goldConverted` already means "earned gold that is no longer the player's to spend": conversions and reservations add to it, `spendable = goldEarned - goldConverted` everywhere, and a career reset sets `goldEarned = goldConverted` (the main-app `resetPlayer` and Knights' `restart.service.ts`), which keeps spent gold, forfeits the rest and needs no change. Purchases, conversions and resets all serialise on the same row lock, so two simultaneous buys cannot spend the same gold. The cost is that `goldConverted` no longer says "converted to Buzz" alone: the shop splits it by purchase kind (`balance.ts`: `converted`, `spent`, `onItsWay`).

## Membership weeks (main app)

The shop sells a perks-only week of the membership the main site sells for Buzz. `grantBuzzPurchaseMembership({ userId, tier, days, idempotencyKey })` in `subscriptions.service.ts` is the grant half of `purchaseMembershipWithBuzz` (which debits Buzz and then runs the same `commitBuzzMembership`, always for one month):

- **What it grants**: an upsert of the `CustomerSubscription` in the `buzzPurchase` slot (`BUZZ_MEMBERSHIP_SUBSCRIPTION_TYPE`) with `cancelAtPeriodEnd` true and `currentPeriodEnd` `days` on (7 from the shop; one calendar month when `days` is absent, as an older Knights build sends it, which is why the main app deploys first), the monthly badges `deliverMonthlyCosmetics` gives that tier (in the same transaction; it is limited to `provider = 'Civitai'`, the same grant prepaid and code-redeemed Civitai memberships get, and its SQL joins every active product at or below the subscribed tier's `level`, so a Gold week carries the Bronze, Silver and Gold badges by design, as any Gold membership of that provider does), then the subscription cache bust and the Freshdesk tier sync (a Freshdesk failure is swallowed: the grant is already committed). No monthly Buzz. `/user/membership` shows it like a Buzz-purchased membership, with the tier and end date.
- **Which product**: the active Civitai product with `buzzPurchase: true` and the item's `tier`, on its monthly 1-interval price. The catalog item carries only the tier, so it needs no price id and works the same in every environment.
- **Rules**: one period at a time, and only when the user holds no live membership of any kind (any `CustomerSubscription` that is not canceled and has not run out). Otherwise the grant is refused.
- **Idempotency**: the key is stored in the slot row's metadata (`grantKeys`, the last 20, kept across a repeat buyer's upserts). The key is looked up first, before the product and before any refusal, and again under the user-row lock inside the transaction, so a retry after a successful grant answers `already` (the cache bust is repeated) and a replay never gets a refusal or a second period, even when the member has since let it lapse and bought another.
- **Refusals and retries**: `BuzzMembershipRefusedError` (`active-membership`, `unavailable`, `not-monthly`) is the endpoint's 409, after which Knights releases the gold. Everything else is a 5xx and stays pending for the tick.

Knights refuses up front too: the shop card reads "You are a member" and `buyItem` refuses `has-membership` before reserving gold, using the same live-membership test (`hasActiveMembership`). The endpoint stays the authority. A membership item whose `days` is not 7 is not sold (`isSellable`, `MEMBERSHIP_DAYS`). The inventory shows the longest-running live membership's tier and end date.

## Equipping

Knights never writes `equippedAt`. `POST /api/mod/knights/shop/cosmetics` (`x-webhook-token`) takes `{ action: 'refresh' | 'equip' | 'unequip', userId, cosmeticId }` and calls the main app's own `equipCosmetic` / `unequipCosmeticByType`, so ownership, event-decoration and withheld rules, the cosmetic caches and the worn-cosmetic queue all run, and the main site shows the item at once. Stickers are used, not equipped. A refusal is a 409 whose message the inventory shows.

## Switches and entry points

`KNIGHTS_SHOP_DISABLED=true` (also `1`, `yes`, `on`) closes the shop and hides its entry points (header gold popover "Visit the shop", the avatar menu and home tile "Shop"); `/shop` then says it is not open. `KNIGHTS_CONVERSION_DISABLED` keeps its meaning for Buzz packs only. The restart-career dialog now lists "Gold you have not yet spent".

## Layout and try-on (SH1, SH5)

`/shop` shows the Buzz packs, then a full-width row of membership weeks, then the cosmetics grouped into shelves by type (`groupCosmetics` in `lib/shop-view.ts`: Badges, Avatar frames, Stickers, Nameplates, then Other), four tiles a row on desktop and two on phones (`CosmeticTile.svelte`). A tile shows the cosmetic's image large; a nameplate tile paints the viewer's username in the nameplate's gradient instead (`nameplate` on the card, validated from `data.gradient` by `nameplateGradient` in `server/shop/cosmetics.ts`; a malformed gradient falls back to the image). Selecting a tile opens `TryOnDialog.svelte` (a bottom sheet on phones): the viewer's profile card with the frame around their avatar (`frameOffsets`, scaled from a 96px avatar by `frameStyle`, as the main site does), their name in the nameplate's gradient, the badge beside it, or the sticker large, plus a sample comment. Its Buy runs the same pre-checks as a tile's Buy (`buyClick`) and then hands over to the purchase confirm dialog; locked items show the rank that unlocks them and owned ones say Owned.

## Motion (M9, M10)

`routes/shop/+page.svelte`, `ShopCard.svelte`, `CosmeticTile.svelte`, `PurchaseDialog.svelte`, `inventory/+page.svelte`. The purse counts down (`tween`, 360ms) only after the server has answered, the dialog fades out, the OWNED seal stamps (`mo-stamp`) and a floater rises; any click or key during the 720ms jumps to the end state. Cards stagger in (first six, 40ms apart), the deal card sheens once on load and every 8s, a Buy pressed without the gold shakes twice and fades in the shortfall line (a held player's Buy does the same with the hold refusal, `refusalText('held')`, and opens no dialog), an equipped item cross-fades to verdigris with the check drawing in. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the count lands at once, there is no floater, shake or sheen, and the rest are 150ms fades. The coin clink of the design is not built: there is no sound asset for it yet.
