---
title: Request removal of intimate images of you
description: How to ask Civitai to remove an intimate image or video of you that was posted without your consent, under the TAKE IT DOWN Act.
---

If an intimate image or video of you (real or AI-generated) was posted on Civitai without your consent, you or someone authorized to act for you can ask us to remove it. Under the TAKE IT DOWN Act, we remove valid requests as soon as possible and no later than 48 hours after we receive them, and we make reasonable efforts to remove identical copies.

Your request should include:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. a link to, or enough information for us to find, each image or video;
3. a brief statement that you believe in good faith it was published without your consent, with any information that helps us confirm that; and
4. how we can contact you.

Submit it by email to [digital-likeness@civitai.com](mailto:digital-likeness@civitai.com).
